At home I run the AI Workbench: a platform where AI‑agents do real IT work. The city is just the interface. Behind it run my own servers, my own network and a team of agents with fixed roles.

What this is about

AI agents are programs that complete tasks on their own: writing code, checking servers, researching, sorting documents. They are becoming part of corporate IT right now. I already run such a team in everyday life, on my own hardware, with everything that comes with it.

  • What it isA platform for 13 AI agents: take jobs, assign them, get them done, check them. I say what I need. An admin agent hands the work to the team and checks every result before it reaches me.
  • Why I build itReading about agents is not enough. I want to know how to run them safely and affordably: who may do what, what it costs, what happens when things fail, who keeps control. You only learn that in real operation.
  • What counts on the jobThe same foundations as in any company: virtualization, a network with VLANs, backups, access and permissions, monitoring, automatic self-tests. Plus the experience of where AI helps and where a simple script is better.

    Minik: a hologram that lives in a phone In progress

    My newest project, born on 3 November. Minik is a small character who lives as a hologram on an old iPhone on the desk. He listens to his name, recognizes people by face and voice, talks freely in many languages and learns something new every night.

    The prototype runs at our home every day. The Android version already runs entirely on a Pixel, everything on the phone. It is turning into an app for everyone: install it, enter your own Gemini key, done. After that, Minik comes to the PC as a character without a background.

    Language models in my own house

    The agents work with cloud models. In case a provider goes down or a limit kicks in, my own models are ready as a backup on the AI PC. They run behind an interface the agents already know. One chat agent once ran fully local as a test, including tool calls.
